Looking Back at My Progress

Reflecting on where I started, what I have learned, and where I am headed next.

📅 July 2026 • Reflection • Growth • Web Development

Looking back on this project, I can honestly see how much my understanding of web development has grown. At the beginning of the course, concepts like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, and website deployment all seemed like separate pieces. As I completed this assignment, those pieces finally started to connect and make sense.

I learned that creating a website involves much more than simply writing code. Developers also need to organize their files, use version control with Git, store projects on GitHub, test their work, and deploy completed websites so they can be viewed online. Every step is an important part of the overall development process.

One thing this project reinforced is that problem-solving is one of the most important skills a developer can have. Throughout the assignment, I encountered small issues with file organization, navigation links, and deployment. Instead of giving up, I learned how to troubleshoot each problem and continue moving forward.

As I continue my education at Full Sail University, I look forward to learning more about responsive design, APIs, React, databases, and full-stack development. This project reminded me that every assignment builds on the last one, and each completed project brings me one step closer to reaching my goal of becoming a professional web developer.
